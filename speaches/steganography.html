Some text on steganography

Have you ever seen those life hacks where they use lemon juice to write down a message and would say “meet at twelve o’clock” after you iron them? This is the so-called “steganography”. In Greek, this word comes from steganos, meaning concealed, and graphein, meaning writing. This is the practice of “Concealed writing”. Since this exists, how would you know now what you see is truly what you see? When you see an advert, is it real? Would some information be hidden, such as “no refunds”? This is why it is important. It changes our perception of reality, and it encourages us to find and seek out what is the most important part, or hidden part of a message. But it is not always used to escape refunds as well.
